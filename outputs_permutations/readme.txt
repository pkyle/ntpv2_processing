The scenario names can be decoded as follows:
d1, d2, d3: electricity demand - reference, high, low
p1, p2, p3: gas prices - reference, low, high
g1, g2, g3: gas-electric power plant costs - reference, low, high
r1, r2, r3: renewable-electric power plant costs - reference, low, high
n1, n2: nuclear-electric power plant costs - reference, low
